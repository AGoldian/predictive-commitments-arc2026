## Commit, act, then allocate

The method builds on [Franzen's solution](https://github.com/da-fr/arc-agi-3-solution) and [Duck](https://github.com/Tufalabs/duck-harness). Before dispatching an action, the model may forecast frame change, level completion and attempt failure. Omission is permitted abstention. The host binds that prediction or abstention to the exact action and scores valid forecasts once against returned public observations. Unavailable labels stay missing; RESET/undo and refused actions remain separate. Solver logic uses no environment implementations, hidden state, answers or game-specific solution rules.

![Prediction commitment, dispatch, public observation, outcome resolution and the next allocation ticket.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AGoldian/predictive-commitments-arc2026/85043878d6c0e085e058d1adc71a8bc0c7c8ed87/paper-draft-20261029/figures/method-loop.gif)

The last eight eligible actions provide paired forecast/outcome histories. Four arms separate the questions:

- B0 keeps baseline prompts, batch policy and priority under common accounting/admission.
- B1 adds the optional prediction interface and keeps baseline priority.
- A1 learns allocation from common progress, coverage and paired probability/outcome moments.
- P uses the same common features and adds three event Brier means.

A1/P predict current-level completion within 8,192 further target-output tokens or 20 nonautomatic actions. These observational labels do not identify the causal benefit of extra compute. At each admission boundary, the scheduler freezes a multiplier of 0.5 plus the fitted probability; upstream baseline priority may still refresh. P falls back to A1 when all event residuals are missing. Capacity is two request/tool exchanges.

## What the controls can tell us

Brier equals mean(p²)+mean(y)−2mean(py). Its interaction can encode pairing beyond the matched marginals, but Brier and that interaction span the same linear predictor space. Fixed L2 shrinkage depends on the chosen basis. Any eventual difference would concern this representation under fixed regularization.

Constant-q diagnostics refit derivative A1_q/P_q pairs, replacing common forecast moments too. They do not compare against original A1. Added training columns are zero at q=0.5 and signed outcome duplicates at q=0.25/0.75 when training variance is positive. Duplication halves the coordinate's effective penalty and doubles its gradient step; it adds no pairing information or policy evidence. Earlier [prediction-verification work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05138v2), [related work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00834v1) and a [calibration-priority plan](https://github.com/dcw06/ARC-AGI-3/blob/54e129671e1583e958dbde2c34aede1a7d341b72/docs/ARC-AGI-3_Project_Plan_7.md) limit novelty claims.

## The DEV feasibility result

The matched pilot used four exposed public games, request seed 101, 1,800 seconds gameplay and 6,000 seconds from first code per arm, including a 3,600-second startup ceiling. Equal ceilings did not produce equal consumed costs. Host-accounted target-output totals were 446,084/412,843/390,586/404,393 for B0/B1/A1/P; GPU-FLOPs and draft work remain unavailable.

A1's disclosed JSON-format adjudication preserved its original refusal and changed no model or runtime.

The fitting collection had only 16 windows, with all three positive endpoints from one game/episode. Public-game exposure, concentrated labels, missing forecasts and one coupled DEV fleet per arm limit interpretation. Request seeds do not establish independent worlds or statistical replicates.
